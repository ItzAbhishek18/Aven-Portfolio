01 — ABOUT

I DON'T JUST WANT TO USE THINGS. I WANT TO UNDERSTAND THEM.

I'm a second-year Software Engineering student exploring the space between technology, design, and the physical world.

I like software, but my curiosity doesn't stop at a screen. I'm fascinated by cars, motorcycles, architecture, games, and the systems behind them.

When something catches my attention, I tend to take it apart, ask questions, experiment, and figure out how the pieces work together.

I've built systems ranging from a taxi booking platform to a vehicle management system, while also exploring visual design through things like logo creation.

Right now, I'm expanding my skills in software development, drawing, and Japanese.

Eventually, I want to build products of my own — and maybe something much bigger.
